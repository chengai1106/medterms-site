Frequently asked

Does MedTerms work offline?
Yes. Everything works with no internet connection. There is no account and no sign-up.

What is MedTerms Pro?
A one-time purchase that unlocks all 347 terms and every quiz. No subscription. You can restore it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the upgrade screen and tap “Restore purchase.”

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

MedTerms is built to respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

None. MedTerms does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your study progress (which terms you have marked as “mastered”) and your Pro purchase status are stored locally on your device. This never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes this data.

Purchases

MedTerms Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase validation is handled entirely by Apple’s StoreKit.

Network Use

MedTerms works fully offline and contains no third-party SDKs or trackers.

Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
