MedTerms for Android

Support & Privacy · Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy covers MedTerms for Android, developed by CHENG QIAO. It describes the app's handling of study records, Google Play purchases and support correspondence.

Support

For help, purchase restoration questions, feedback or bug reports, email medterms.support@gmail.com.

About MedTerms

MedTerms is a medical vocabulary study aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, or provide medical advice or emergency guidance. For questions about your health, contact a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Study without an account

The app does not require sign-up and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The terminology library works offline. The app does not send your study progress to the developer. It does not ask for personal medical records or access Health Connect, location, contacts, camera or microphone data.

Data stored on your device

MedTerms stores which terms you have mastered or are still learning, your study positions and introduction preferences locally on your device. The Reset progress action removes the local learning records and study positions. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. This Android version does not enable app-data cloud backup or cross-platform sync.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time MedTerms Pro purchase using your Google account. The app queries purchase status, verifies the signed purchase record and stores a signed receipt locally to support offline access to Pro. The receipt is excluded from app backup. The developer does not receive payment card details through the app. Google processes purchase and account data under its own privacy policy.

An internet connection is needed to buy, restore or refresh purchase status. Restoring a purchase requires the Google Play account used for that purchase. Apple App Store purchases cannot be restored in the Android app.

External references

If you open a reference link, the linked website opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies. References include MedlinePlus, OpenStax and the National Cancer Institute. MedTerms is independent of these publishers.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the contents you choose to send, and uses that information to respond to your request. Please do not send personal medical information. You may contact the same support address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable record-keeping obligations.

Children

The app does not ask users to submit personal or health information. The Google Play listing will specify the intended audience and content rating.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Questions may be sent to medterms.support@gmail.com.
