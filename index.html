Frequently asked

Does studying work offline?
The terminology library and locally stored progress work offline. An internet connection is required to load or restore MedTerms Pro and to open external reference links. There is no account or sign-up.

What is MedTerms Pro?
An optional, one-time purchase that unlocks all 347 terms, complete study decks, and focused category quizzes. The free experience includes 10-card previews, Quick Quiz, and progress tracking. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open a locked category, then tap “Restore purchase” on the MedTerms Pro screen.

Medical Information

For education only. MedTerms is a vocabulary study aid. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or emergency guidance. Medical terms can have different meanings depending on context.

For questions about your health, contact a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

MedTerms is built to respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

None. MedTerms does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your study progress — including which terms are marked as mastered or still learning, plus where you left off in a study deck — is stored in the app’s local preferences. MedTerms does not transmit this progress to the developer, and the developer cannot access it. Device backups and restores, if enabled, are handled by Apple and iOS under your device and iCloud settings.

Purchases

MedTerms Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase validation is handled entirely by Apple’s StoreKit.

Network Use

The terminology library and progress work offline. If you choose a citation link, your device opens the referenced website in your browser; that website's privacy policy applies. Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple through StoreKit. MedTerms contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and the developer does not receive or store your personal information from these interactions.

Children's Privacy

MedTerms does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults. Age suitability is shown in the app's App Store listing.
